Brand Brief

Brand Story

Born from a passion for clean, results-driven skincare, the brand exists to deliver dermatologist-grade formulations without the inflated pricing of legacy beauty houses. Every product is built around proven actives — niacinamide, retinol, hyaluronic acid — and tested against real skin concerns, not marketing trends.

Founded in 2024 by a small team of formulators and clinicians, the brand has grown from a single hero serum into a curated line of 12 SKUs trusted by over 50,000 customers across the region.

Brand Promise: Honest formulas. Visible results. No nonsense.

Target Audience

Three core personas drive 80% of revenue:

01 · PRIMARY

The Routine Builder

Women 25–34, urban, beauty-literate. Researches ingredients before buying. Active on TikTok and Instagram.

02 · GROWTH

The First-Time Investor

Women 20–25 graduating from drugstore products. Price-sensitive but quality-curious.

03 · LOYALTY

The Sensitive Skin Veteran

Women 30–45 with specific concerns (rosacea, melasma). High AOV, repeat purchasers.

Tone of Voice

We speak like a knowledgeable best friend — confident, clear, never condescending. We explain the science but skip the jargon. We use humor sparingly and never at the customer's expense.

  • Confident, not arrogant
  • Honest, even when it's uncomfortable
  • Specific over vague — actual percentages, real timelines
  • Inclusive — every skin type, every shade, every story

Visual Identity

Clean editorial layouts. Generous white space. Photography that's clinical-meets-warm — real skin, real textures, no over-retouching.
